"I don't sell houses in general — I sell homes in Central and West Auckland, and I've done it long enough to know what actually moves buyers here."

I've spent over 15 years working these suburbs — Titirangi, Blockhouse Bay, Lynfield, New Lynn, Green Bay, Glen Eden, Kelston, Mount Roskill, New Windsor, Glendene and Avondale. Not a general patch on a map — the specific streets, school zones, and buyer types that decide what a home in your suburb is really worth.

For most of that time I've worked alongside my husband and business partner Peter. Long enough that locals still call us "THE A-TEAM" — a nickname that stuck because of how we work, not a slogan we invented. These days that same hands-on partnership operates under the Raine & Horne name, backed by a national network but run with the attention of two people who actually know your street.

What that means for you: no handoff to a junior agent once the sign goes up, no generic script. You get me, from the first walk-through to the final signature.
